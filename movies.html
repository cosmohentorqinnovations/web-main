Synopsis

STOPWATCH follows Arjun, a young engineer who discovers a stopwatch capable of resetting time by exactly one hour. When his closest friend dies in a freak accident right before his eyes, Arjun uses the stopwatch to go back — over and over — each time believing this reset will be the one that saves her. But time, it turns out, has its own rules. And the more Arjun resets, the more he loses of himself.

A meditation on grief, guilt, and the price of second chances, told through the lens of Tamil Sci-Fi.

About the Production

STOPWATCH is CosmoHentorq's first original film production — a deliberate extension of the company's belief that technology and storytelling are not separate disciplines. The film is written, directed, and produced entirely in-house, with a Tamil-first focus, representing an early foray into Indian Sci-Fi short film.

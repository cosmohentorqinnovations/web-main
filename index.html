Ask Us Directly
What is CosmoHentorq Innovations? +
CosmoHentorq Innovations Private Limited is a Startup India and Startup TN recognized IT company incorporated on May 4, 2024, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. We build transformative technology across AI, cloud services, and robotics to empower businesses and individuals.
What services do you offer? +
We specialize in Research & Development, Artificial Intelligence, UI/UX Design, and Cloud Computing. Our products include JULIE (AI platform), HAK (cloud storage), YAKO (kitchen robotics), and PULSAR (augmented reality).
How can I contact CosmoHentorq? +
You can reach us via WhatsApp at +91 8754883872, or email us at labs@cosmohentorq.com for research & lab inquiries, or apex@cosmohentorq.com for executive matters. We are located at 250, Malleeswari Nagar, Madambakkam, Chennai, Tamil Nadu.
What is your approach to project management? +
We follow agile methodologies with a strong emphasis on collaborative development, rapid iteration, and transparent communication. Each project has dedicated ownership with regular milestone reviews to ensure quality and timely delivery.
Do you offer custom solutions? +
Yes, absolutely. We design and develop custom technology solutions tailored to your specific business needs — from bespoke AI integrations to custom cloud infrastructure using our HAK platform. Contact us to discuss your requirements.
What industries do you serve? +
We serve a wide range of industries including healthcare, education, food-tech, enterprise software, logistics, and retail, or any sector that can benefit from AI, cloud infrastructure, or intelligent automation.
